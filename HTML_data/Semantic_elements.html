Semantic Elements

In this page, we will go through and practice different semantic elements.

Elements: i-idiomatic, em-emphasis, b-bring attention to, strong, dl-description list, dt-description term, dd-description detail, blockquote, q- inlinequote.

1) i- idiomatic element

Examples:

  1. In Japnese, word arigatou gozaimasu! means "thank you very much! - foreign language.
  2. In One piece world, people who use conqurer's haki rule the New world - technical word.
  3. She wondered silently,"where is he going?" - A thought
  4. Jack drowned in ship name Titanic in 1912- ship name.
  5. Human spiecies have evolved from Homo Sapiens - scientific word

2) em -emphasis

Examples:

  1. Remember! no matter what you should not open the gate.
  2. To see more of my content Please subscribe my channel!
  3. Never thought the we could lose the game.

3) b- bring attention to

Examples:

  1. In our shop, we sell hp, Dell, Lenove, Asus company laptop.
  2. Name: Sudip Patra, Address: lokmat square, Gender: Male.

4) strong

Examples:

  1. Note: Always use capital letter for "P"
  2. Warning: Do not enter this room.
  3. We need AB+ blood for patient in room no. 112 urgently.

5) dl- description list, dt- description term, dd- description detail

Examples:

The Big three Animes:

One piece:
One piece is a story of a boy named Monkey D. Luffy where he is on a adventure to find the greatest treasure and become King of the Pirates.
Naruto:
Naruto is a story of a boy named Uzumaki Naruto where he aspires to be a great Hokage and end all the hatred in the World.
Bleach:
Bleach is a story of a boy named Kuroski Ichigo where he accidently got exposed to the Soul society world and now to protect his world and his friends, he have to protect Soul society.
